# H-M12 — H-M6 + Futures OI/Price Alignment

## Statement
Within H-M6 events, the combination of prior three-bar price movement aligned with the breakout direction AND a positive three-bar OI change may identify a more favorable movement/MAE profile.

## Exact descriptive definition
For event direction d:
- aligned 3-bar price movement: d * (Close_t - Close_{t-3}) > 0
- OI build: OI_t - OI_{t-3} > 0
- aligned_build = both conditions true
- H-M12 = H-M6 AND aligned_build
