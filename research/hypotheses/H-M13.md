# H-M13 — Future-Only Prior-Extension × Event-Shock Interaction

## Statement

Within the frozen H-M1 event sequence, prior directional extension does not have a universal effect. Its relationship with later movement and fixed-risk tradeability appears to depend on the magnitude of the current breakout shock relative to the preceding local range.

The test is:

**H-M1 lifecycle stage = prior directional extension × current event shock/release intensity.**

## Why this hypothesis exists

The external NIFTY26JANFUT contract showed large prior directional displacement before H-M1 events and negative subsequent tradeability.

The older 2015-2021 NIFTY index transfer independently showed weaker H-M1 outcomes in the highest prior-5 displacement tercile.

However, Apr-Sep 2026 NIFTY futures show the opposite pattern: the highest prior-5 displacement tercile has the strongest movement and R2/R3 point estimates.

Therefore prior extension alone cannot be the rule.

## Prospective test

On a genuinely fresh futures sample:

1. Keep H-M1 unchanged.
2. Calculate signed prior-5 and prior-20 displacement using only completed bars.
3. Calculate current event shock relative to the preceding local range.
4. Test the interaction as a continuous feature, not a fitted hard cutoff.
5. Evaluate 5/10/20-bar returns, MFE/MAE and the frozen 1R/1.5R/2R/3R first-passage outcomes.
6. Preserve contract and day clustering.
7. Do not choose thresholds after seeing the test outcomes.

## Status

POST-HOC, FUTURE-ONLY, NOT VALIDATED.
