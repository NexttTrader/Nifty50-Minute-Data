# H-M2 — Directional / Regime Asymmetry of H-M1

## Statement
The H-M1 effect may be materially dependent on market regime and breakout direction. In the Apr-Sep 2026 NIFTY futures sample, the strongest validation behaviour appeared in downward compression-release breakouts, particularly when the prior short-term trend was already bearish.

## Evidence observed
Jul-Sep 2026 validation:
- prior downtrend + down breakout: H-M1 n=25, mean 20-bar directional return +2.226 ATR vs -0.160 controls; H-M1 benchmark R2 +0.442 vs -0.182 controls.
- prior uptrend + up breakout: H-M1 n=25, mean 20-bar directional return -0.615 ATR vs +0.052 controls; H-M1 benchmark R2 -0.305 vs +0.090 controls.

Cross-instrument 2015-2021 index data does not reproduce the same strong asymmetry consistently, so the current observation may be specific to the 2026 market regime and/or futures instrument.

## Status
**UNVALIDATED EXPLORATORY HYPOTHESIS**

## Governance
H-M2 is frozen as a test hypothesis. It must not be used to alter H-M1 on the Apr-Sep development/validation data. It should only be evaluated on unseen futures contracts or forward observations.
