# H-M5 — Session Timing

## Statement
H-M1's gross executable behavior may vary by intraday timing.

## Current evidence
In the 2026 futures sample, validation candidate mean 3R was strongest in the first two hours of the session and weaker in several later bins. The pattern is post-hoc and subgroup sample sizes are small.

## Status
UNVALIDATED.

## Governance
No session-time filter should be used in any live/forward rule until frozen separately and tested on unseen data.
