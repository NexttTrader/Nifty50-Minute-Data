# Phase 57–64 — Deep Research Summary

## Scope
These phases use the exact, audited SALMA-B0 and the frozen H-M1 event universe on Apr-Sep 2026 NIFTY futures. No parameters were changed for H-M1.

## Main findings
1. H-M1's strongest effect is on medium-horizon movement persistence (10–20 bars), not immediate momentum.
2. H-M6 (H-M1 + >=2 direction-aligned SALMA clipping bars in the prior 3) gives the strongest current SALMA-specific result, but its fixed 1ATR/2R trading edge remains uncertain and friction-sensitive.
3. SALMA is not proven unique: generic smoothers can also improve the compression-release event. SALMA's unique clipping component remains the most distinctive mechanism.
4. Completed 15-minute state agreement improves candidate outcomes, but generic 15-minute MA agreement produces similar conditioning; this is a multi-timeframe state effect, not proof of SALMA specificity.
5. Session context, prior-day extremes, gaps, and opening-range state show exploratory conditioning effects, but none is promoted without holdout.
6. Propensity matching and contract-level analyses keep point estimates positive for H-M6, but confidence intervals remain wide.
7. A compact interaction model combining SALMA flip, clipping count and their interaction gives small additional discrimination over the base event. The gain is not strong enough to deploy.
8. Timing-placebo tests support the specificity of a 1–3 bar pre-breakout SALMA window; farther windows are generally weaker.
9. Risk geometry indicates that H-M1/H-M6 events can have larger favorable excursion and lower adverse excursion, but not every event can monetize this with a fixed 1ATR stop.

## Governance
H-M1 and H-M6 remain frozen. Exploratory H-M8 and later interaction features must not be threshold-tuned on Apr-Sep before unseen/forward testing.
