# NIFTY / SALMA Research Laboratory

This repository is the official version-controlled home for the NIFTY Futures / SALMA v3.0 research project.

Current status (2026-09-30):
- SALMA-B0 is frozen at the user-supplied RedK SALMA v3.0 formula.
- Current research dataset: 8,594 five-minute candles across six actual NIFTY monthly futures contracts, Apr-Sep 2026.
- Raw SALMA direction/swing is not a validated standalone trading strategy.
- H-M1 is the current frozen hypothesis worth external and forward validation.

Research principles:
1. No look-ahead.
2. Historical predictions are immutable.
3. Facts, definitions, hypotheses and evidence remain separate.
4. Every experiment is logged.
5. Discovery, validation and holdout periods remain distinct.
6. No broad parameter/filter mining after a hypothesis is frozen.
7. Actual futures contracts are preferred for trading-system validation.
8. No profitability claim without unseen validation and realistic execution assumptions.

See research/hypotheses/H-M1.md for the frozen H-M1 specification.
